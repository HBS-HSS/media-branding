What Tail-Ed Care delivers

Medication Records (eMAR)

Six-status MAR with real-time logging, refusal capture, alerts and CQC-aligned audit trail.

Rota & Shift Management

GPS-verified clock-in/out, drag-drop calendar, swap & cover requests, two-way RotaCloud sync.

Service User Records

17-tab care record, 8-section person-centred plans, risk assessments & All-About-Me.

Incidents · Accidents · Damages

Three CQC-aligned record types with structured workflows, ABC charts & safeguarding routes.

Smart Manager

Rules-based findings queue — only the items that need a manager. 20+ rule types, click-through.

Secure Chat & Notifications

Team · family · whistleblowing channels with voice notes, presence & auto-translation.
