Platform

Three primitives. Everything else composes from them.

Tasks describe work. Agents execute it. Hierarchies organise both. The rest of the system is plumbing — and we keep it out of your way.

Hierarchies, not flat queues.

Work belongs in trees. Hierarchy lets you nest scopes, inherit policy, and route by level — no more flat queues with surprise priorities.

Agents that actually finish.

Idempotent execution, structured observability, configurable retry semantics. Agents either finish, escalate cleanly, or surface the exact reason they stopped.

Observability you can act on.

Latency percentiles, claim graphs, escalation paths. Every metric ties back to a request ID — debug an incident with the dashboard, not a vibe.

Security by construction.

SOC 2 Type II, scoped tokens, per-tenant audit trails, regional data residency. Pen-tested quarterly; reports on request.

Predictable performance.

Median 120 ms claim latency, p99 under 400 ms. Burst-safe queues with clear backpressure semantics — your tail-end never blocks the head.

Integrates with your runway.

SDKs for Node, Python, Go, .NET, Dart. CLI for ops. Webhooks for everyone else. We don't ask you to rewrite your stack to talk to ours.
