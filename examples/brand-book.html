01 · Foundations

Who we are, in three adjectives.

Hierarchy Technologies builds technology products with structure, clarity, and engineering rigour. The brand expression sits at the intersection of order and circuitry — captured visually by the H mark, whose terminal nodes evoke a circuit board.

Personality

TraitWe are…We are not…

ModernGeometric, restrained, currentTrendy, ornamental
ConfidentDirect, plainspoken, decisiveBrash, salesy
HumanHelpful, calm, respectfulCute, ironic, cloying

Visual signature

Three things should be immediately recognisable as HT, even when stripped of context:

  • The H mark. Geometric H with circular node terminals.
  • The brand gradient. Azure → indigo → violet, left-to-right.
  • Wide-tracked display type. Wordmark uses generous letter-spacing.

Audience

Engineers, technical leaders, and operators integrating Hierarchy products into mission-critical systems. They read fast and skim first; they prefer specifics over adjectives; they are skeptical of marketing fluff. Write and design accordingly.

02 · Logo system

The lockup.

The Hierarchy logo is a three-element lockup: the H mark, a vertical separator, and the wordmark. At small sizes, the lockup pieces drop in this order: separator → wordmark → leaving the H mark alone.

02 · Logo system

Misuse.

The logo is fixed art. Don't redraw, recolor, distort, or compose against it. Specific prohibitions:

  • Don't recolor the wordmark or change its weight.
  • Don't apply effects (drop-shadow, glow, bevel, outline-stroke).
  • Don't rotate, skew, or stretch.
  • Don't separate the gradient mark from the wordmark unless using the dedicated hierarchy-mark.svg variant.
  • Don't place the gradient on backgrounds in the azure-200..violet-200 range — contrast collapses.
  • Don't crop the H mark; it relies on the four nodes being visible.
  • Don't outline or fill the inner counter of the H.
  • Don't use the wordmark without the mark on hero surfaces (use the lockup).

08 · Design tokens

One source. Five outputs.

Authored once in W3C DTCG JSON; consumed by every stack via the matching derivative.

FileFormatConsumer
tokens/tokens.jsonW3C DTCGSource of truth
tokens/tokens.cssCSS custom propertiesWeb — Angular global styles
tokens/tokens.scssSCSS variables + mapsWeb — SCSS partials
tokens/tokens.tailwind.cjsTailwind presetAny Tailwind consumer
tokens/tokens.dartDart constantsMobile — Flutter ThemeData
tokens/tokens.tsTypeScript constantsWeb — typed access

Token categories

  • color.brand — azure / indigo / violet, 50–900 ramps
  • color.neutral — 0–1000 (ink at 900)
  • color.semantic — success, warning, danger, info
  • color.surface / text — themed light/dark aliases
  • color.gradient.brand — canonical 3-stop gradient
  • font — family, weight, size, lineHeight, letterSpacing
  • space — 4-pt scale 0…24
  • radius / shadow / breakpoint / motion

Updating

Edit tokens/tokens.json first, then mirror the change to derivatives. Run preview.html to verify visually. The full branding/ folder is the master.
